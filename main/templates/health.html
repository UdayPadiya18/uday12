Krishna School of Pharmacy And Research

Krishna School of Pharmacy & Research (KSP), Constituent School of Drs. Kiran & Pallavi Patel Global University (KPGU), Vadodara is approved by Pharmacy Council of India. It offers Bachelor of Pharmacy and Master of Pharmacy in four disciplines (Pharmaceutics || Pharmaceutical Quality Assurance || Pharmacology || Pharmaceutical Technology), and PhD program in Pharmaceutical Sciences. Our B. Pharm. Program is NBA re-accredited till 30.06.2025. KSP emphasizes strong foundations, research, and industry connections.

Krishna Ayurved Medical College

Krishna Ayurved Medical College (KAMC), Constituent School of Drs. Kiran & Pallavi Patel Global University (KPGU), Vadodara offers B.A.M.S. degree with comprehensive Ayurvedic education and practical training at Matrushri Davalba Ayurved Hospital. Approved by NCISM & Department of AYUSH, it fosters high standards in teaching, research, patient care, and rural health awareness.

Krishna School of Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Krishna School of Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation prioritizes enhancing students' clinical reasoning, innovation, and dedication to health. With excellent facilities and enthusiastic faculty, we provide an ideal environment for skill development. Recognizing the evolving healthcare landscape, we emphasize the importance of continuous career advancement and innovation among healthcare professionals.
